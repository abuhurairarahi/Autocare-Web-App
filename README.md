# 🚗 Vehicle Workshop & Repair Tracking System

A web-based vehicle workshop management system designed to streamline service bookings, repair tracking, mechanic assignments, spare parts inventory, invoicing, and communication between vehicle owners and workshop staff.

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Vehicle Workshop & Repair Tracking System** provides a centralized platform for managing vehicle servicing and repair operations. It connects administrators, workshop managers, mechanics, and vehicle owners through role-based functionality while allowing guests to explore available services and workshop information.

The system aims to make vehicle maintenance more organized and transparent by providing repair progress updates, cost estimate approvals, service history, and digital invoices.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Simplify vehicle service appointment booking.
- Improve coordination between workshop managers and mechanics.
- Provide transparent repair estimates and approval workflows.
- Track repair progress from diagnosis to completion.
- Manage spare parts inventory and service pricing.
- Maintain vehicle service histories and digital invoices.
- Enable communication between vehicle owners and workshop staff.
- Generate workshop performance and revenue reports.

## 👥 User Roles & Features

### 1. 🔐 Administrator

- Add and remove workshop managers.
- Approve mechanic and vehicle owner accounts.
- Manage service categories, including engine, electrical, denting, and servicing.
- Manage spare parts inventory and prices.
- Monitor workshop performance statistics.
- Generate monthly revenue and service reports in PDF format.
- Broadcast service offers and notices.

### 2. 🏢 Workshop Manager

- Receive and manage service booking requests.
- Create job cards for approved bookings.
- Assign mechanics to repair jobs.
- Send repair cost estimates for vehicle owner approval.
- Approve spare parts usage for repairs.
- Track repair progress and delivery dates.
- Generate PDF invoices.
- Communicate with vehicle owners and mechanics through chat.

### 3. 🔧 Mechanic

- Log in and view assigned job cards.
- Update repair stages:
  - Diagnosis
  - Repairing
  - Testing
  - Ready
- Record spare parts used and labor hours.
- Upload before-and-after repair photos.
- Report additional faults discovered during repairs.
- Communicate with workshop managers.

### 4. 🚘 Vehicle Owner

- Create an account and log in.
- Register vehicles with model and registration number.
- Book vehicle service appointments.
- Approve or reject repair cost estimates.
- Track live repair status.
- View service history for each registered vehicle.
- Download PDF invoices.
- Chat with workshop managers.

### 5. 🌐 Guest

- Browse available services and service rates.
- View workshop locations and operating hours.
- Access workshop contact information.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Role-Based Access:** Separate functionality for administrators, workshop managers, mechanics, vehicle owners, and guests.
- **Service Booking:** Manage vehicle service appointments and booking requests.
- **Job Card Management:** Create repair jobs and assign mechanics.
- **Repair Tracking:** Follow the repair lifecycle from diagnosis to ready-for-delivery status.
- **Cost Estimation:** Allow vehicle owners to approve or reject repair estimates.
- **Inventory Management:** Track spare parts, prices, and usage.
- **Service History:** Maintain a record of services performed on registered vehicles.
- **PDF Documents:** Generate invoices and monthly service and revenue reports.
- **Communication:** Provide chat functionality between relevant users.
- **Notifications & Offers:** Broadcast service offers and notices.
- **Low-Stock Alerts:** Support alerts for spare parts that need replenishment.

## 🔄 Repair Workflow

The system follows a structured repair process:

1. The vehicle owner registers a vehicle and books a service appointment.
2. The workshop manager reviews the booking request.
3. An approved request is converted into a job card.
4. A mechanic is assigned to the job.
5. The workshop manager sends a repair cost estimate to the vehicle owner.
6. The owner approves or rejects the estimate.
7. The mechanic performs the repair and updates its progress.
8. Parts usage, labor hours, photos, and additional faults are recorded as needed.
9. The repair progresses through diagnosis, repairing, testing, and ready stages.
10. The workshop manager generates an invoice, and the vehicle owner can download it.
